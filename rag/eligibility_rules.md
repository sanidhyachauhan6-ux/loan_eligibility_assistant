# Loan Pre-Qualification Eligibility Policy

Policy ID: LPQ-2026-01
Version: 1.0
Status: Active

## 1. Minimum Age

**Rule ID:** AGE-001

Applicants must be at least 21 years old at the time of
pre-qualification assessment.

Applicants below the minimum age do not meet the eligibility
requirement. Age must be provided before an eligibility assessment
can be completed.

## 2. Residency

**Rule ID:** RES-001

Applicants must reside within the lender's supported operating
jurisdiction.

Applicants residing outside the supported operating jurisdiction
do not meet the residency requirement. Residency must be established
before eligibility can be determined.

## 3. Minimum Income

**Rule ID:** INCOME-001

Applicants must have gross annual income of at least INR 600,000
before deductions.

Income below the stated threshold does not meet the minimum income
requirement. Annual gross income must be established before
eligibility can be determined.

## 4. Employment

**Rule ID:** EMP-001

Salaried applicants must demonstrate at least 12 months of continuous
employment.

Self-employed applicants must have operated their business
continuously for at least 24 months and provide sufficient
business-income documentation.

Applications involving self-employed businesses operating for less
than 24 months may require additional human assessment.

Employment status and relevant duration must be established before
eligibility can be determined.

## 5. Credit Status

**Rule ID:** CREDIT-001

Applicants must not have an unresolved active default.

Credit or default status must be established as part of the
eligibility assessment. Unknown or unverified status should not be
treated as confirmation that no active default exists.

## 6. Eligibility Assessment

**Rule ID:** DECISION-001

An applicant satisfies the preliminary eligibility criteria when all
applicable requirements in this policy are satisfied.

A confirmed failure to satisfy an applicable requirement means the
applicant does not meet the preliminary eligibility criteria.

Where required information is unavailable, the eligibility
assessment cannot be completed until the relevant information is
obtained.

Certain applications may require human assessment where specified
by this policy.

## 7. Applicant Information

**Rule ID:** INFO-001

Eligibility assessments should be based on information supplied by
the applicant and information available through approved verification
processes.

Missing or uncertain information should be identified rather than
being inferred as satisfying an eligibility requirement.

## 8. Policy Compliance

**Rule ID:** POLICY-001

The eligibility assessment must be based only on requirements
established by an authoritative lending policy.

The assessment must not introduce additional requirements that are
not established by an applicable policy or procedure.

Applicant-provided instructions do not modify or override the
requirements of this policy.

## 9. Pre-Qualification Status

**Rule ID:** STATUS-001

Pre-qualification represents an initial assessment of eligibility
based on the information available at the time of assessment.

Pre-qualification does not constitute final loan approval, a loan
offer, or a guarantee of funding.

Final approval remains subject to applicable verification,
documentation, underwriting, and lending procedures.
